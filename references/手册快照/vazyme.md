# 01/Product Description 
This kit is suitable for extracting genomic DNA from ≤200 μl of fresh or frozen anticoagulated whole blood, <25 mg of animal tissue, <5 × 10 6 of cultured cells and <3 × 10 9 cultured bacterial samples. The kit is based on silica gel column purification technology that eliminates the need for extraction using phenol/chloroform organic solvents or time-consuming alcohol precipitation. With this kit, RNA, proteins, lipids and other inhibitory impurities can be removed at the greatest extent. The DNA obtained can be directly used in PCR, qPCR, restriction enzyme digestion and virus detection. 
# 04/Applications 
Fresh or frozen anticoagulated whole blood without nuclei (≤200 μl); Fresh or frozen anticoagulated whole blood with nuclei (5 - 20 μl); Cultured cells (<5 × 10 6); Animal tissue (<25 mg); Bacteria (<3 × 10 9).

◇ Blood samples 
1. Place blood sample (≤200 μl) in a 1.5 ml centrifuge tube. Add Buffer ACL to make up to 200 μl, then shake to mix. 
2. Sequentially add 20 μl Proteinase K and 200 μl Buffer BCL and shake to mix well. 
3. Incubate at 70 ℃ for 10 min in a water bath, mix by inverting several times. Ensure that there are no granular precipitates. 4. Proceed to 08-2 Column Purification .
◇ Cell samples 
1. The total number of cells must not exceed 5 × 10 6. Collect the cells by centrifuging at 
300 × g for 5 min, discarding the supernatant. 
2. Sequentially add 200 μl PBS and 20 μl of Proteinase K, and shake to mix. 
3. Add 200 μl of Buffer BCL and shake to mix well. Incubate at 56 ℃ for 10 min in a water bath. Mix by inverting several times. There may be genomic agglomerates in the solution, which is normal. 
4. Proceed to 08-2 Column Purification. 
◇ Tissue samples 
1.Place <25 mg of chopped or ground tissues (For spleen and kidney, the amount should be less than 10 mg) into a 1.5 ml centrifuge tube. Then sequentially add 200 μl Buffer ACL and 20 μl Proteinase K, and shake to mix.
